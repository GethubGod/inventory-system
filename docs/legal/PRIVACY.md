# Babytuna Systems Privacy Policy

Effective August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Babytuna Systems handles information through its mobile app, web pages, and related services (the “Service”). Babytuna Systems is operated by the developer identified on its App Store listing (“Babytuna,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). The Service is built for authorized restaurant teams, not for general consumer use.

## 1. Who controls workplace information

The organization that invites you generally decides why its workplace information is collected, who may use it, and how long it should be kept. Babytuna processes that information to provide the Service. Managers and administrators may view and manage information in their organization’s workspace.

Initial onboarding normally begins online through a manager-created invite. The invite opens the app, where you can create a PIN or password. Legacy account methods may also use an email address or access code.

## 2. Information we handle

Depending on the features your organization enables, we may handle:

- Account and team information, such as your name, optional email address, role, assigned location, enabled modules, account status, invite status, and sign-in identifier.
- Authentication and security information, such as hashed PINs or passwords, session tokens, failed sign-in attempts, device details, and security logs. We do not store readable PINs or passwords.
- Workplace records, such as inventory items and counts, storage areas, orders, quantities, notes, suppliers and supplier contact details, fulfillment activity, checklists, reminders, notification status, tip-entry records, and related timestamps.
- Content you choose to provide, such as profile images, photos, voice input, corrections, and messages or notes.
- Device and technical information, such as app version, operating system, push-notification token, diagnostics, IP address, browser or device type, and basic usage events needed to operate and secure the Service.

We do not require payment to use the Service and do not collect payment-card or bank-account information for it.

## 3. Microphone, camera, and photos

The Service accesses the microphone, camera, or photo library only when you choose a feature that needs it and grant device permission. Voice features may send audio or a transcription to our service providers to interpret an inventory count, order, or tip entry. Camera and photo features may process an image for the action you requested. The Service is not designed to record audio continuously.

Avoid including private conversations or unnecessary personal information in voice, photo, note, or order content. You can deny or later revoke device permissions, although the related feature may then be unavailable.

## 4. How we use information

We use information to:

- create and secure accounts and complete manager-authorized onboarding;
- provide inventory, ordering, fulfillment, checklist, reminder, team-management, and tip-entry features;
- synchronize records across authorized users, locations, devices, and web tools;
- deliver notifications and help managers administer access;
- process optional voice or image requests;
- troubleshoot errors, prevent abuse, investigate security issues, and maintain reliability;
- improve features and user experience using aggregated, de-identified, or appropriately protected information; and
- comply with law, enforce our terms, and protect users, organizations, Babytuna, and others.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use workplace information for third-party targeted advertising, and the Service does not include third-party ad trackers.

## 5. When information is shared

Information is shared only as reasonably needed for the following purposes:

- Within your organization. Managers, administrators, and authorized team members may see records based on their role, location, and enabled modules.
- Service providers. We use providers for database hosting, authentication, web hosting, app distribution, notifications, diagnostics, and optional voice or image processing. These providers process information for us under their applicable terms and safeguards.
- Organization-directed actions. Information may be sent to a supplier, messaging app, or other destination when an authorized user chooses to send or share it.
- Safety and legal reasons. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, prevent fraud or harm, protect rights and security, or enforce agreements.
- Business changes. Information may be transferred as part of a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate confidentiality and this Policy or notice of a replacement policy.

We do not share personal information with data brokers or advertisers for their own marketing.

## 6. Retention

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to provide and secure the Service, support the organization’s operations, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Retention varies by record. For example, active account and workplace records may remain while an organization uses the Service; security logs and backups may remain for a limited period; and an organization may retain order, inventory, fulfillment, or tip records after a user leaves.

When information is no longer needed, we delete, de-identify, or securely isolate it according to our operational practices. Backups may take additional time to expire.

## 7. Your choices and rights

You may be able to review or update account details in the app. You can control camera, microphone, photo, and notification permissions in device settings. Where available, you can request account deletion from the app. You may also ask your manager or organization administrator to correct, export, restrict, or remove workplace records.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to know, access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information, or to object to or limit certain processing. You may also have a right to appeal a denied request or complain to a privacy regulator. We will not discriminate against you for making a valid privacy request. Some requests may need to be handled by your organization because it controls the workplace record, and some information may be retained where law or legitimate business needs permit.

For a privacy request, contact your organization administrator first or use the developer contact method available through the Babytuna Systems App Store listing or website. We may need to verify your identity and authority before acting.

## 8. Account deletion

Deleting an account removes the authentication account and personal access where supported. It may also delete or detach profile, notification, and device-token information. Records needed for the organization’s legitimate operations may remain, may be reassigned, or may be retained in de-identified form. A consumed invite remains marked as consumed to prevent reuse, even if the associated user reference is removed.

## 9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. These include access controls, role and location restrictions, encrypted network connections, hashed app credentials, rate limits, and restricted server credentials. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Children

The Service is a workplace tool and is not directed to children under 13. Organizations should not invite anyone who is not legally permitted to use the Service for workplace duties. If we learn that a child’s personal information was provided improperly, we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

## 11. International processing

Information may be processed in the United States and other places where our service providers operate. Those locations may have different privacy laws. Where required, we use appropriate measures for cross-border transfers.

## 12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as the Service or law changes. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice when a change is material. Earlier versions may be kept for reference.

## 13. Contact

For workplace-data questions, contact your manager or organization administrator. For privacy questions, use the developer contact method available through the Babytuna Systems App Store listing or the Babytuna Systems website.
