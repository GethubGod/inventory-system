Variant 02 · Exception Desk

Resolve what needs attention.

An exception-first operations desk elevates suspected mistakes above all routine data and compresses the rest into a chronological audit feed. It trades warmth and leisurely scanning for urgency, fast correction, and explicit separation of sensitive access controls.

Variant 03 · Workbook

Everything in one working surface.

A spreadsheet-minded console maximizes comparison, keyboard-era familiarity, and the amount visible without scrolling. It trades softness and guided pacing for compact controls, explicit columns, and direct access to every management action.
