A1 · gratuity + scope

  • Labelled Gratuity — not "auto-grat". Third well, visually identical to Cash and Card: no green, no tint of any kind. Blank is legal and means 0. No 18%-of-subtotal calculator.
  • All three amounts carry a rule under the value that turns accent-red on focus. That underline is the only thing marking them as typeable — it is not decoration.
  • Scope switch sits directly above the wells with no label over it, defaults to Whole day on dinner, and is hidden entirely on lunch.
  • On Whole day: subtract today’s recorded lunch field by field (cash−cash, card−card, grat−grat) at this location. The receipt shows entered → −lunch → records.
  • Save stores both the typed figures and the derived shift figures.

B1 · partial shares

  • Badge on each selected chip: tapping cycles 100 → 75 → 50 → 25 and wraps. Tapping the chip body still toggles the person on/off.
  • Pool is distributed by weight: full share = pool ÷ Σweights. Kenji at 50% raises everyone else from $41.00 to $45.56.
  • “What each person takes” only renders when at least one share is under 100%. All-full nights show nothing but the strip.
  • Strip label: Split N ways when even, Full share when uneven.
  • Largest-remainder allocation — the shares must sum to the pool exactly, to the cent.

C1 · note + edge cases

  • Dashed “+ Add a note” under the strip → textarea (280 max, live counter) → Cancel / Attach → one-line preview with an edit affordance. Empty text on Attach collapses back to the dashed button.
  • Never required; no note = no column noise on the dashboard.
  • The note’s confirm button reads Save, not "Attach".
  • Lunch not on record + Whole day: show the closer nothing — no banner, no extra tap. It still saves flagged with reason day_total_no_lunch for the manager. Nothing at the register can fix it, so it is not the closer’s problem to read.
  • Subtraction goes negative: the one warning that does show — red, with Save disabled until it is fixed.
  • Saved screen: the same three wells as the entry form (Cash · Card · Gratuity, same grid, same type) but read-only — no underline, since nothing on it is typeable. Holds 10 seconds with a visible countdown, then hands the phone back to the scan gate; Done skips the wait.

Columns

  • Existing columns keep their existing widths and tints. Two additions only: a caret column at the left and a note/action cell at the right.
  • Cash cell gains a day −lunch badge when the entry was recorded from a whole-day total; hover shows the raw figure.
  • “Split between” shows N names + a 1 partial mark when any weight < 1; “Per person” shows the full share with a full share caption instead of a flat average.
  • Gratuity gets no top-level column — it lives in the detail panel and the CSV.

Detail panel

  • Three cards: how this number was reached (raw → −lunch → recorded, then card + grat), who takes what (every name, weight, dollars, and a Pool total that must equal the cash pool), note (author + time, or “No note on this entry.”).
  • Flagged rows: keep the red tint and Verify; a flagged row auto-opens its detail when the range first loads.
  • Fix dialog gains gratuity, the scope switch, per-person weights and the note.

Everything else

  • CSV export gains auto_grat, entered_scope, raw_cash, raw_card, note, and a per-person weight column.
  • Overview take-home-by-person must use the same weighted allocation, not cash ÷ split_count.
  • Voice entry is unchanged in this phase: it still fills cash / card / people only.
